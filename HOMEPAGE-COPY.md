**Lead-in:**
An AI assistant can read your product pages. But it doesn't know your member prices, your bulk discounts, what ships where, or who's allowed to buy what. So it guesses — and guesses cost you sales.

## Section 3 — How it works (three steps, plain language)

**Section heading:** A translator between your store and AI shoppers

**Lead-in:**
You already have the rules. RetailAgentOS explains them in a language AI understands — before checkout, not after it fails.

**Step 1 — Your store introduces itself.**
A simple, standard file tells AI assistants what your store offers and what it can do — like a storefront sign written for machines.

**Step 2 — Your rules become answers.**
Who qualifies for member pricing. What the bulk discount is. What ships where. Each rule becomes a clear yes, no, or here's-what-you'd-need — answered instantly, while the AI is still browsing.

**Step 3 — Every decision comes with a reason.**
When the answer is no, the AI hears why and what would change it. No silent failures, no abandoned carts, no guessing.

**Kicker:**
Same question, same answer, every time. There's no AI guessing inside RetailAgentOS — your rules run exactly as you wrote them, and we can prove it.

## Section 4 — The readiness ladder

**Section heading:** How ready is your store? Four levels. Most stores are at zero.

**Level 1 — Visible.** AI can find you. Your store shows up when AI assistants go shopping. *For any store — takes about a day.*

**Level 2 — Correct.** AI gets your rules right. Prices, promotions, memberships, and shipping — answered correctly before checkout. *For growing stores with real pricing complexity.*

**Level 3 — Trusted.** AI can buy safely. The price the AI shows is the price you charge. Age-restricted and regulated products handled properly. *For grocery, wholesale, and regulated retail.*

**Level 4 — Native.** Your whole platform is ready. Commerce platforms build it in once — every store on the platform inherits levels 1–3 automatically. *For platforms and marketplaces.*
